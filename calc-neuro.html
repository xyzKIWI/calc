tPA (Alteplase) Dosing for Ischemic Stroke Calculator
tPA (alteplase) 中風劑量

領域：神經｜來源數：1
tPA dosing is calculated by the following: Dose, mg = 0.9*(Weight, kg) Administer 10% of the total dose as an initial IV bolus over 1 minute and the remainder infused over 60 minutes. Waste, mg = 100 - (Bolus, mg + Infusion, mg) Note: Total maximum dose is 90 mg.
⚠ 適用限制｜岡山 tPA 組套（Kiwi 2026-08-22 提供）：Actilyse (T.P.A.) 2ACTIL ST ASOR ×1 ＋ Sodium Chloride 0.9% 100 mL IVD；組套另含 post IV tPA NIHSS 檢查與護理紀錄（開始／結束時間）、導尿、胃管。組套未載明每公斤劑量，劑量依 Rosen 10e c087／仿單：0.9 mg/kg（max 90 mg），10% bolus over 1 min，其餘 60 min。高醫未備 tenecteplase，該計算機已移除。
來源：MDCalc；Alteplase package insert.
MDCalc：https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/3940/tpa-tissue-plasminogen-activator-dosing-stroke-calculator

ABC/2 Formula for Intracerebral Hemorrhage Volume
ABC/2 腦出血體積估算

領域：神經｜來源數：2
Volume of Hemorrhage = A × B × C × Slices / Hemorrhage Shape A = Length B = Width C = Slice Width # Slices with Hemorrhage Present Hemorrhage Shape: If Round/Ellipsoid: 2; Otherwise: 3. (See references below for this update to the formula.)
來源：MDCalc；Kothari RU, Brott T, Broderick JP, et. al. The ABCs of Measuring Intracerebral Hemorrhage Volumes. Stroke.1996; 27: 1304-1305. doi: 10.1161/01.STR.27.8.1304.
MDCalc：https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/2031/abc2-formula-intracerebral-hemorrhage-volume

5B: Right arm motor drift
6A: Left leg motor drift
6B: Right leg motor drift
7: Limb Ataxia
8: Sensation
